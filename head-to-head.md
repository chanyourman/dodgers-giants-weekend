# Head-to-Head History & Oracle Park

## All-time rivalry

- **Regular season:** Dodgers lead 1,294–1,292–17 (via Wikipedia). The Dodgers took the all-time lead in September 2025 — the first time they've led the series since August 8, 1896.
- **Including postseason:** Dodgers lead 1,297–1,294–17.
- **Postseason:** Dodgers lead 3–2 (2021 NL Division Series, won by LA 3–2).
- **Total meetings:** 2,608, dating to May 3, 1890 at the Polo Grounds (New York era).
- **Largest victories:** Dodgers 17–0 (Sept 13, 2014); Giants 20–2 (May 6, 1903 and Sept 10, 1938).
- **Longest win streaks:** Dodgers 10 (1953); Giants 12 (1937–38).
- **Since 1970:** Dodgers are 517–436 (.543) vs the Giants.

## 2026 season series: Dodgers lead 6–4

- Sept 18–20 @ Dodger Stadium: Dodgers sweep — 8–2, 10–4, 3–1.
- Sept 25–27 @ Oracle Park: the final three games of the regular season.

## Oracle Park — one of MLB's toughest places to hit

- **HR park factor 77** (2024–26 window, Covers) — the lowest in baseball. Cold marine air, the deep right-center gap, and bay-side conditions knock down fly balls.
- **Runs factor ~0.83–0.96, HR factor ~0.81–0.83** (FantasyPros, three-year rolling).
- Per Statcast: **4th-worst park for hitters overall, 2nd-worst for home runs** in 2026.
- **Dimensions:** 339' to left, 391' to center, **421' to right-center (Triples Alley)**, 309' to right, with a 24-foot wall in right field.
- The deep alleys inflate triples (3B factor above 1.10) while crushing home run rates — warning-track power goes to die here, especially at night in cool air.
